## About Us

StartPlaying is the largest online platform connecting players with professional Game Masters (GMs) for tabletop roleplaying games (TTRPGs) of all kinds, including Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, and many more. The company’s mission is to make finding and joining games easy, safe, and accessible for everyone—eliminating the need to search through forums or social media. StartPlaying enables players to explore new adventures, learn game systems, and join welcoming gaming communities, while supporting GMs in building careers and fostering belonging through play.
